About Student Task Organizer

Keep track of every deadline

About this project

Student Task Organizer is a simple web app that helps college students keep track of schoolwork in one place, instead of scattered across chat messages, notes, and memory.

What you can do

  • Add tasks with a title, subject, due date, priority, and optional notes
  • Mark tasks as done, edit them, delete them, and undo a delete
  • See overdue and due-today tasks clearly marked, with friendly labels like "Due tomorrow"
  • Search, filter by status, and sort by due date, priority, or subject
  • Check the dashboard for your progress, your next deadlines, and your tasks by subject
  • Export a backup of your tasks, or your deadlines as a calendar file
  • Use light or dark mode, and install the app to use it offline

How it works

The site is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no frameworks or accounts. Your tasks are saved in your own browser and are never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Known limitations

Tasks live only in one browser on one device, so clearing browser data or switching devices means starting over. Use the backup export regularly, and import the file to restore your tasks or move them to another device.
