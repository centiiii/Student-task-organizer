Privacy Policy

Keep track of every deadline

Last updated: October 2026

What this site stores

Student Task Organizer saves the tasks you enter so they are still there when you come back. For each task this means the title, subject, due date, priority, optional notes, and whether it is done. It also saves your light or dark mode choice.

Where it is stored

This information is saved only in your own web browser, using a feature called localStorage. It stays on your device.

What this site does not do

  • It does not send your tasks to any server or third party.
  • It does not require an account or ask for personal information.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising.
  • It does not load third-party scripts, fonts, or trackers.

Offline use

The site can be installed on your device and used without an internet connection. To make this work, your browser saves a copy of the site's own pages, styles, and icons on your device. This copy does not contain your tasks.

Backup and calendar files

The Backup & export section can create a backup file of your tasks and a calendar file of your deadlines. These files are created in your browser and saved to your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere by this site. They are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust. If you import a calendar file into an online calendar service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Notes field

Notes are saved in your browser and included in backup and calendar files. Please do not enter passwords or other sensitive information in your tasks or notes.

Removing your data

You can delete tasks one by one with the Delete button, remove finished tasks with Clear completed, or remove all stored data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as visit times and IP addresses, according to its own privacy statement. This site's code does not collect or use that information.

Limitations

Because your data lives only in your browser, clearing your browser data, or switching to another browser or device, will remove access to your tasks. Use the backup export regularly to keep a copy.
