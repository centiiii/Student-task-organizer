Privacy Policy

Keep track of every deadline

Last updated: [October 2026]

What this site stores

Student Task Organizer saves the tasks you enter (title, subject, due date, priority, and whether the task is done) so they are still there when you return.

Where it is stored

This information is saved only in your own web browser, using a feature called localStorage. It stays on your device.

If you use the backup features, your data may also be saved to a file on your device.

What this site does not do

  • It does not send your tasks to any server or third party.
  • It does not require an account or ask for personal information.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising.

Removing your data

You can delete tasks one by one using the Delete button, or remove all stored data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Limitations

Because data lives only in your browser, clearing your browser data or switching to another device or browser will remove access to your tasks.
